### From a folding table to a movement

In the winter of 2007, a few Rivertown families noticed neighbors going without and decided to do something about it. What began as a single food drive in a church basement has grown into five programs serving thousands of people every year.

We're still powered by the same belief: that a community takes care of its own. Every program we run is shaped by the neighbors who use it and delivered by the volunteers who love this town.
