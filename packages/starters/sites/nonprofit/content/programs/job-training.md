Our Job Training program helps adults build the skills and confidence to land steady, living-wage work. Free workshops cover résumés, interviewing, digital skills, and the trades, while our employer partners offer paid apprenticeships and direct hiring pipelines. Coaches stay with each participant from first class to first paycheck.
