The Rivertown Community Garden turns three vacant lots into rows of vegetables, herbs, and flowers tended by neighbors of every age. Families grow their own food, kids learn where it comes from, and a share of every harvest goes straight to the food pantry. It is part classroom, part kitchen, and part town square.
