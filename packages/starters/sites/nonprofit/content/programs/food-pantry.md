The Riverside Food Pantry keeps shelves stocked with fresh produce, pantry staples, and household basics for anyone in Rivertown facing food insecurity. Volunteers pack boxes each morning and welcome more than 400 families a week — with a choice-based market that lets neighbors pick what their household actually needs.
