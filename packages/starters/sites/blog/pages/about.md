The Long Field started as a folder of half-finished notes — things I wanted to think through but had nowhere to put. Eventually the folder got big enough that I gave it a name and a front door.

It is not a content strategy. There is no posting schedule I feel guilty about missing, no growth target, no funnel. It is a place to write slowly and in public about the small number of things I actually care about: clear language, quiet software, and the discipline of finishing work properly.

## What you'll find here

Most essays land in one of three fields. **Design** — how things are made, and why so much of it is made badly. **Technology** — the tools we use to think, and the ones that think for us. And **craft** — the unglamorous, essential work of revision, attention, and doing a thing until it is right.

I write for people who would rather read one careful essay than ten quick takes. If that's you, welcome.

## How it's made

This site is built with Jx and published as plain, fast HTML. No trackers follow you around, no popups interrupt you, and the whole thing is designed to get out of the way so you can read. That's the point of the place.
