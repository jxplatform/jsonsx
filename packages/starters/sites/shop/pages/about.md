### From a garage bench to Spoke Avenue

Cadence started in 2009 as a two-person repair bench in a drafty garage. Word got around that we'd fix anything, explain what we did, and never upsell you a part you didn't need. Fifteen years later we've grown into a full shop — but that bench mentality hasn't changed.

We stock bikes we'd ride ourselves, from featherweight road machines to a kid's very first pedal bike, and every one gets fitted and safety-checked before it rolls out the door.
