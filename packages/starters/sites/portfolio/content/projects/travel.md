Three weeks along a coastal range, documenting landscape and the small human marks within it — a fishing hut, a footpath, a lone figure against the weather. We travel light and shoot early, trusting the hour when the light does the composing for you.

The series is a study in scale and silence, printed large so the horizon has room to breathe.
