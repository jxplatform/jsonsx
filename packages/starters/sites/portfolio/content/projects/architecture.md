An architectural study of a brutalist civic building, commissioned for the firm that restored it. We returned across several days to read how the light moves over the concrete — hard geometry at noon, softening into something almost tender at dusk.

The brief was simple: show the structure honestly, and let its proportions carry the frame. No wide-angle theatrics, just clean lines and patient observation.
