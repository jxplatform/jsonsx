A portrait series built on patience and a single soft window. We keep the setup minimal so the attention stays where it belongs — on the face, the posture, the small tells that make a likeness feel true.

Whether for founders, artists, or families, each sitting is a short collaboration: we talk, we adjust, and we wait for the frame that finally looks like you.
