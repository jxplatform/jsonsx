An unhurried summer celebration among the rows, photographed from first light through the last dance. We work quietly at the edges of the day, letting the real moments — a held breath, a grandmother's tears, a spontaneous toast — unfold without direction.

The result is a wedding archive that feels less like a set of poses and more like a story you can step back into: warm, textural, and honest to the people in it.
