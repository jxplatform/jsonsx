A commissioned menu shoot for a neighborhood kitchen, photographed in the rush of a working service rather than a sterile studio. Steam, char, and the honest imperfection of a plate handed across the pass — that is the texture we were after.

We styled lightly and shot fast, chasing the exact moment a dish looks its most tempting before it goes out the door.
