### Rooted here since 2004

Northshore Realty started with a single office on Lakeshore Drive and a simple promise: treat every client like a neighbor, because most of them are. We grew by word of mouth, one closing and one referral at a time.

Today we're a team of local agents who still answer their own phones. We know which streets flood, which schools are worth the premium, and what a home is really worth — not just what a listing says.
