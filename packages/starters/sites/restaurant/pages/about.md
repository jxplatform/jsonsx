### From a small kitchen, with big intentions.

Bistro & Café started with a simple idea: a room where the coffee is honest, the bread is warm, and nobody rushes you out the door. We took over an old storefront on Market Street, put in a wood counter and a proper oven, and started cooking what we'd want to eat.

Nine years later, the regulars have become friends, the growers have become partners, and the menu still changes with whatever's best that week.
