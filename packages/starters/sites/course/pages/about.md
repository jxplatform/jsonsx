### Built by engineers who wanted a better way in

Craft & Code Academy started with a frustration: most people who want to code get stuck in a maze of half-finished tutorials. So we built the program we wish we'd had — project-based from day one, with mentors who've shipped real software and a cohort that learns together.

We keep classes small on purpose. When you get stuck, someone is there. When you ship, someone celebrates. That's how people actually make it from curious to career.
